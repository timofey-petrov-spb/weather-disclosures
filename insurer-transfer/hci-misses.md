# Why the prior extractor missed 19 HCI disclosures

Two original filings contain 23 individually labelled monetary facts attributable to Hurricane Ian. The previous method automatically recovered four distinct amounts. Of the 19 others, 17 received no proposition for the source figure, one correct value was held for review, and one was held at the wrong scale. Each row below identifies one of the 19. Amounts are USD millions at the precision reported by the company. These measures overlap economically and should not be added.
